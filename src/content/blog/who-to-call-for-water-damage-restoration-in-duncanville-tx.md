## Who Should I Call First for Water Damage in Duncanville?

Call a water damage restoration company first, not your insurance company. Your insurer needs a damage assessment before they can do anything useful, and every hour without extraction and drying increases the total loss. MCC Restoration and Contracting Services serves Duncanville and the surrounding Best Southwest area, including neighborhoods like Swan Ridge and Cedar Ridge. Reach them at (682) 772-9123.

That said, the restoration company is not always the very first call. Run through this quick triage:

1. **Is the water source still flowing?** If a pipe is actively spraying or a supply line is still running, you need a licensed plumber to stop it first. The restoration crew extracts and dries; they do not repair plumbing. Shut off your main water valve at the meter if you can reach it safely, then call a plumber and a restoration company at the same time.
2. **Is there an electrical or gas hazard?** Standing water near electrical panels, outlets, or appliances is a 911 situation. Do not enter the area. Call emergency services, then your utility provider, before anyone else enters the space.
3. **Is the water source stopped and the space safe?** Call MCC Restoration at (682) 772-9123. This is the most common scenario: a burst pipe that has been shut off, an appliance line that failed overnight, or a roof leak after a North Texas hailstorm.

Call your insurance company after the restoration crew has assessed the damage. Most adjusters want a written scope and moisture readings before they open a claim file anyway. The restoration company generates that documentation.

## When Is It a Plumber vs. a Restoration Company?

A plumber fixes the source of water. A restoration company fixes what the water did to your home. You almost always need both, and the order matters.

Call a plumber when:
- A pipe is still actively leaking or has not been shut off
- You suspect a slab leak under the foundation (a plumber locates and repairs it; the restoration crew handles the resulting water damage)
- A water heater has failed and needs replacement
- A toilet or drain line is backing up and needs a rooter or repair

Call a restoration company when:
- The water source is stopped and you have standing water, wet flooring, or wet drywall
- A roof leak has let water into the ceiling or attic
- A dishwasher, washing machine, or refrigerator line failed and soaked the floor
- A previous water event was not fully dried and you now see staining, warping, or smell mildew

For slab leaks specifically, which are common in Duncanville due to the expansive clay soil, the typical sequence is: plumber locates and repairs the leak, then MCC Restoration handles the water damage to flooring, drywall, and subfloor. Some homeowners try to skip the restoration step to save money. That works until the hidden moisture grows mold. North Texas summers are humid enough that mold can establish in as little as 24 to 48 hours in wet building materials. ## What Should I Do While I Wait for the Crew?

A few steps in the first 30 minutes protect your home and your insurance claim.

- **Stop the source** if it is safe. Shut the main water valve or the supply valve under the affected fixture.
- **Document everything** before moving anything. Take photos and video of standing water, wet flooring, wet walls, and any damaged contents. Your insurance adjuster will want this.
- **Move valuables and electronics** out of the wet area if you can do so without entering standing water near electrical sources.
- **Do not run fans or your HVAC** to try to dry things out. Household fans move air but do not remove moisture from the structure, and running HVAC through a wet space can spread contamination and mold spores. Leave the drying to equipment designed for it.
- **Do not use a wet/dry shop vac** on Category 2 or Category 3 water. You can use it on clean water from a supply line, but gray or black water requires proper containment and disposal.
